# AI usage

**What it shows.** What the AI provider was asked to do and what it cost, from Crowdin's record of every billed AI call: tokens and an estimated cost in USD per call. Two reports read it: **Tokens usage raw data** (one row per call) and **Costs by users** (one total per member). This is the AI provider's bill. It is not Translation cost's AI match rows: when someone asks what a word costs with AI or what AI saved, answer from AI in money questions in `references/reports/translation-cost-figures.md`, and use this file when they mean the bill.

**Generating it through the API.**
- Enterprise, organization owners and admins: `tokens-usage-raw-data` (`generate_organization_ai_report_tokens_usage_raw_data`) and `costs-by-users` (`generate_organization_ai_report_costs_by_users`), then `check_organization_ai_report_status` and `download_organization_ai_report`. crowdin.com has the same two reports for the user's own account (`generate_user_ai_report_*`).
- Dates are ISO 8601 and both ends are included; `format` is `json` or `csv` (use `csv` for long periods). Filters: `projectIds`, `promptIds`, `userIds`, and on the raw data `languageIds`. There is no group-by and no workspace filter, and neither report is archived.
- A `projectIds` filter drops calls that have no project (the AI Translate API, AI File Translate, and the AI Gateway and AI Proxy, through which apps such as AI Pipeline call AI); a `languageIds` filter drops calls with no language. For an organization total, leave both out.

**How it counts.**
- *Raw data:* per call `date` (the server's time, with no zone), `inputTokens`, `outputTokens`, `totalCost` (USD, empty when no price was known), `projectId`, `promptId`, `promptName`, `promptAction`, `source`, `aiProviderId`, `aiModelId`, `useSystemCredentials`, `userId`, `applicationName` and `targetLanguageId`. Retried calls are billed and included.
- *Costs by users:* one `totalCost` per member, empty costs counted as 0, with no split by project, prompt or source. For a split by project, sum the raw data by `projectId`, map projects to workspaces (`list_projects`, `list_groups`), and show calls without a project as their own line. Never say AI spend cannot be split by project.
- *Which calls are translation.* `source` names the feature that made the call: `pre_translate:manual` and `pre_translate:workflow` are AI pre-translation, `ai_suggestion` is AI suggestions in the Editor, `ai_translate_strings` and `ai_file_translate` are the AI Translate APIs; `ai_proxy` and `ai_gateway` are calls made by apps, shown as their own line by `applicationName` and counted as translation only for a translation app such as AI Pipeline; `qa_check`, `ai_alignment`, `advisor` and `ai_prompt_completion` are not translation. A prompt or prompt action alone does not separate pre-translation from Editor suggestions: both run a pre-translation prompt, often the same one.
- *When the data exists.* `totalCost` on Crowdin's credits from December 2025 (older calls were filled in at the price of that time); on the organization's own provider keys (`useSystemCredentials` false) only from 2026-07-07, so older calls there have tokens and no cost; for custom AI providers (apps such as AI Pipeline) from 2026-08-04. `source` from 2026-07-20: before that, pre-translation and Editor suggestions cannot be told apart. `targetLanguageId` from 2026-10-12: there is no AI cost per language before that, and it cannot be rebuilt. For calls without a cost, estimate tokens × today's price from `list_all_supported_ai_models`, labelled as an estimate, or ask for the provider's invoice.
- `list_ai_request_logs` keeps 30 days and `list_ai_usage_members` only the current day and month: they are for single requests and limits, not for spend over a period.

**Money rules.**
- `totalCost` is Crowdin's estimate at the model's list price in USD. On Crowdin's credits it is what was charged; on the organization's own keys the provider bills the customer directly, and the invoice can differ.
- Translation cost never contains AI or MT engine spend, so adding the AI bill to it counts nothing twice. Its currency is a label, though: show AI as a separate USD line, and add it to a Translation cost total only with an exchange rate the user gives.
- Crowdin records no money for MT engines at all. When a full cost is asked, say so, and offer MT volume (Translation activity, Translation Savings), never an MT cost.
